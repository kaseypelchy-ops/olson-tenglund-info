# Auto Value Promotions: Price and Image Notes

All shown prices are transcribed from the six user-supplied flyers, with item-code variations displayed separately within each family card. The source calls some prices **Jobber Specials**; these may be trade-only and must not be represented as confirmed public retail pricing. Always confirm local availability and customer eligibility before publishing the page as a retail offer.

The product-card photos are **external URLs** from publicly visible product listings. They are genuine product listing photographs rather than crops of the supplied flyer. They are not owned or licensed by this site; obtain supplier/manufacturer-approved product assets for long-term publication and replace URLs as needed. Photo references are kept per product in `data/promotions.json`. Some products without a verifiable exact photograph use a text placeholder rather than an incorrect image. Do not substitute unrelated photos for an exact SKU.
