PARTS HELP & PRACTICAL ADVICE

How to find the right auto part.

A few details about your vehicle can make it easier to identify the correct replacement part. Here's what to gather before you call or visit a parts counter.

Home/Parts help

Start with your vehicle information

Many components look similar but differ by engine, trim, production date, or equipment package. Gathering this information can help narrow down the correct part.

  1. Year, make, and model

    Know the model year, manufacturer, and exact vehicle model. For example, a part that fits one model year may not fit another.

  2. Engine and configuration

    For engine-related, electrical, cooling, and other application-specific parts, engine size, drivetrain, or trim may matter. Check the owner's manual or vehicle information label if you're unsure.

  3. VIN, when needed

    The 17-character Vehicle Identification Number can help distinguish vehicle configurations. You can often find it at the base of the windshield or on the driver's door jamb. Share it directly with the parts professional when necessary, not in a public comment.

  4. Existing part number or a photo

    If accessible and safe to inspect, bring the part number from the old component or a clear photo of its label and connectors. A matching appearance alone does not guarantee compatibility.

  5. What you're replacing and why

    Explain which component you need and any relevant symptoms or diagnostic information. This helps clarify the request, although diagnosing the underlying problem may require a qualified technician.

What to ask at the parts counter

Useful questions: Is this part the correct fit for my vehicle configuration? Are there different options or brands? Is it in stock at this store, or can the team help me understand availability?

Stock, pricing, ordering options, and applicable warranties can vary. Contact your preferred Olson & Tenglund location to confirm current details before visiting.
