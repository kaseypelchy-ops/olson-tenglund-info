Is Olson & Tenglund permanently closed?

No. Olson & Tenglund, Inc. continues operating its auto parts stores in Port Allegany, Coudersport, and Smethport, Pennsylvania.

Is Olson & Tenglund still a NAPA store?

No. Olson & Tenglund is now an Auto Value partner. Older internet listings may still show our previous supplier affiliation.

What are Olson & Tenglund store hours?

All three stores are open Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. They are closed Saturday and Sunday. Holiday hours may vary.
