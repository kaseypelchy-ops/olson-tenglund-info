BUSINESS UPDATE & ANSWERS

Yes, Olson & Tenglund is open.

Still your local parts people. Now a proud Auto Value partner.

Is Olson & Tenglund permanently closed?

No. Olson & Tenglund, Inc. continues operating its auto parts stores in Port Allegany, Coudersport, and Smethport, Pennsylvania.

Is Olson & Tenglund still a NAPA store?

No. Olson & Tenglund is now an Auto Value partner. Older internet listings may still show our previous supplier affiliation.

What are Olson & Tenglund store hours?

All three stores are open Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Weekend hours are not yet confirmed; please call your local store. Holiday hours may vary.

Where is Olson & Tenglund located?

Port Allegany: 303 N Main St, Port Allegany, PA 16743. Coudersport: 112 N Main St, Coudersport, PA 16915. Smethport: 400 W Main St, Smethport, PA 16749.

How do I call Olson & Tenglund?

Port Allegany: 814-642-2517. Coudersport: 814-274-8881. Smethport: 814-887-7777.

Can I order auto parts online?

This informational website provides store information and contact details. Please call your local store to ask about parts availability and ordering options.

Do you help customers find parts for their vehicles?

Yes. Call your nearest Olson & Tenglund store with your vehicle year, make, model, and the part you need. Our team can discuss available options.

Do you serve independent repair shops?

Yes. Olson & Tenglund serves professional repair shops as well as retail customers at its three Pennsylvania locations. Call your local store to discuss your parts needs.

Are parts availability and online ordering listed on this website?

This is the official store information website, not an online checkout. For current stock, ordering, or special requests, call the appropriate store.

Where can I find the official Olson & Tenglund website?

The official website is olsontenglund.com. It includes current store locations, phone numbers, and information about the Auto Value partnership.
