QUICK ANSWERS

Still open. Still local. Now with Auto Value.

Is Olson & Tenglund still in business?

Yes. Our three Pennsylvania stores in Port Allegany, Coudersport, and Smethport are open and serving customers.

Did Olson & Tenglund close when it left NAPA?

No. The business continues operating and is now partnered with Auto Value. Some older directory listings have not yet been updated.

When can I visit?

Each store is open Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Call your local store for weekend or holiday availability.

Can professional repair shops call for parts?

Yes. We serve professional shops as well as retail customers. Contact the nearest parts counter to discuss your needs.
